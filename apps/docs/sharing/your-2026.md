# Your 2026

Your 2026 plays back your year in the Layout Tool as a short story of cards. It shows the drawers you planned, the bins you placed, your busiest month, your longest print and the most complicated bin you designed. Then it gives you a type, such as Night Shift Supervisor, and a summary. It ends with the people who keep the tool free.

## Find it

Your 2026 is open from 1 December 2026 to 15 January 2027. In that time:

- A row that says <Ui k="dock.yearReview.ready" /> sits above your account at the bottom of the sidebar. On a phone, it is in the settings panel. A dot shows until you open it, and the x hides the row.
- What's New has an entry for it, with a button that plays it.

The row shows only if you made something this year: a drawer you started in 2026 with at least one bin in it, or a bin design you made or changed in 2026. If you have nothing from 2026 on this device, the page says so and offers a sample year to watch instead.

## Watch it

The cards play by themselves.

- To go to the next card, tap or click the right side of the card, or press the right arrow key. To go back, use the left side or the left arrow key.
- To pause, hold the card down or press Space.
- To leave, press Esc or click the x.

The cards that print bins show the real bin from your design, turning slowly. On a slow device, or before the 3D engine is on it, they show the bin's picture instead.

Sound is off at the start. Turn on the speaker button for a soft tune. The tune keeps playing into the credits.

## Save a card

Click the save button next to the sound button. It makes a tall picture of the card on screen, sized for a phone story. On a phone, your share sheet opens. On a computer, the picture downloads.

## The credits

The last card thanks the supporters who keep the Layout Tool free. Click <Ui k="your2026.thanks.rollCredits" /> to see their names roll, or <Ui k="your2026.thanks.support" /> to join them.

## Your data

Your 2026 reads only what is already on this device: your layouts, your saved bin designs and your export count. It sends none of it anywhere. If you use sync, sign in on this device first, so your designs from other devices are part of the year.
