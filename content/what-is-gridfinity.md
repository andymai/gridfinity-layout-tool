# What is Gridfinity?

Gridfinity is a modular, open-source storage system designed for 3D printing. Created by Zack Freedman in 2022, it has become one of the most popular organization systems in the maker community.

The system uses a standardized 42mm grid that lets you create custom drawer organizers, desktop storage, and tool holders that fit together perfectly every time.

## How the Grid System Works

Gridfinity is built on a simple but powerful concept: everything fits a 42mm × 42mm grid.

### Base Plates

Base plates are the foundation. They sit in your drawer and provide the grid pattern that holds bins in place. Bases come in various sizes—1×1, 2×2, 4×4, and larger—so you can cover any drawer.

### Bins

Bins drop onto the bases and hold your stuff. They come in different footprints (1×1, 2×1, 3×2, etc.) and heights measured in "units" (U). Each height unit is 7mm, so a 3U bin provides about 21mm of usable storage height.

### The Magic of Modularity

Because everything follows the same grid, you can:

- Mix and match any bin sizes
- Rearrange your layout anytime
- Share designs with the community
- Print only what you need

## Why Makers Love Gridfinity

> **Tip:** Gridfinity works for everything from tiny screws to large power tools. The community has created thousands of specialized bins.

### Fully Customizable

Unlike store-bought organizers, Gridfinity adapts to your stuff—not the other way around. Need a bin that holds exactly 4 AA batteries? Someone's probably designed it. Need something unique? Design your own.

### Print Only What You Need

No more buying a 20-pack of bins when you need 3. Print exactly what your drawer requires, in exactly the colors you want.

### Huge Community Library

Thousands of free STL files are available on:

- [Printables](https://www.printables.com/search/models?q=gridfinity)
- [Thangs](https://thangs.com/search/gridfinity)
- [MakerWorld](https://makerworld.com/en/search/models?keyword=gridfinity)

### It Just Works

The profiled base of each bin mates with the baseplate's raised grid, so bins stay put but can be easily rearranged. No glue, no screws, no fuss.

## Common Questions

### Do I need special filament?

No. Standard PLA works great. Some people use PETG for durability or ASA for heat resistance, but PLA handles most use cases.

### How long does it take to print?

A typical 2×2 bin takes 1-2 hours depending on height. You can print overnight and wake up to a finished organizer.

### Can I use Gridfinity without a 3D printer?

Some makers sell printed Gridfinity parts on Etsy and eBay. It's more expensive than printing yourself, but it's an option.
