# Gridfinity Label Maker

Turn a parts list into labels for your Gridfinity bins. Paste rows from a spreadsheet or an order page, pick your label maker, and download one image for each label.

[CTA: Open the label maker](/labels)

## How it works

1. Type your list, or paste rows from a spreadsheet or an order page.
2. Pick your label maker and the tape or labels in it.
3. Check each label in its preview, then download the images and print them from your printer's app.

## Label makers

| Label maker          | Tape or labels                                           |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Brother P-touch      | 6, 9, 12, 18 and 24 mm tape                              |
| DYMO LabelManager    | 6, 9, 12 and 19 mm tape                                  |
| Niimbot D11 and D110 | 12 × 22, 12 × 40, 14 × 40, 15 × 30 and 15 × 50 mm labels |
| Phomemo D30          | 12 × 40 and 14 × 30 mm labels                            |

Each image has one pixel for each dot of the printer's head, in black and white, so small text prints sharp.

## Paste a parts order

When you paste rows, the label maker finds the column of part numbers. Each part number prints on a second line, in a font where 0 and O look different. On 6 mm tape, the part number prints after the description, on the same line and in the same font. A typed line that starts or ends with a McMaster-Carr part number, such as `91290A113 Socket head screw`, works the same way. The label maker does not look up part numbers, so paste the descriptions with them.

## Sized for your bins

Tape labels can fit their text, or match the width of a bin 1, 2 or 3 grid units wide: 36, 78 or 120 mm. Long descriptions wrap onto two or three lines to fit.

## Print the labels instead

To print a label as part of the bin, use the [bin designer](/designer). It prints text on label tabs, or makes swappable label plates that click into the bin. With a plate, you change a label without a new bin.

More detail is in the [label maker docs](/docs/printing/label-maker).
